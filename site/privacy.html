Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20/09/2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Discord on our website, we receive basic information from your Discord account, such as your user ID, username, avatar, and the roles you hold on the PredCord and Predage Community servers. When you submit a staff application or a ban appeal, we also store the answers you provide in the form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Sign you in and keep you signed in on the website and dashboard;
  • Show you the correct sections of the dashboard based on your roles;
  • Review staff applications and ban appeals;
  • Keep moderation records, such as bans and warnings, for the servers you're part of.

3. Discord Authentication

Signing in on Predage's website uses Discord's official OAuth2 login. We never see or store your Discord password. Discord only shares with us the information you approve during the sign-in process.

4. Cookies And Sessions

We use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in between page visits. This cookie is required for the website and dashboard to function correctly and is not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

5. Data Retention

We keep staff application answers, ban appeal answers, and moderation records for as long as needed to run the PredCord and Predage Community servers properly, or until a staff member removes them.

6. Sharing Of Information

We do not sell your information. Data collected through the dashboard is only visible to authorized staff members with the correct permissions, and is never shared with third parties outside of what's required to operate our Discord servers.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to review or delete the information we hold about you, such as a past application or appeal, by contacting the staff team. Some information, such as moderation history, may be kept for the safety of the community even after a request.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to the staff team on the Predage Community Discord server.
